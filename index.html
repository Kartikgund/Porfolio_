About

Engineering reliable systems for infrastructure that can't go down

I'm a .NET Full Stack Developer based in Pune with 4+ years of experience turning complex, data-heavy infrastructure problems into software that control-room teams rely on every day. I currently work as Assistant Manager – Software Development at Arya Omnitalk Wireless Solutions, where I own the design and delivery of monitoring and automation systems for highway infrastructure end-to-end.

My core strength is real-time systems — using SignalR to push live state to dashboards instead of polling, and PostgreSQL-backed aggregation layers to make sense of high-volume device telemetry across multi-lane deployments. I've built centralised monitoring platforms that track hundreds of ANPR devices, camera feeds and background services simultaneously, and streaming pipelines that convert RTSP camera feeds into browser- and iOS-friendly HLS/MSE formats.

Beyond the .NET stack, I also build with Python — training and deploying computer-vision pipelines with YOLO and PaddleOCR for edge inference on devices like the Raspberry Pi 5, and building Flask-based services for video recording and playback around MediaMTX. Whether the stack is C# or Python, my approach stays the same: understand the data flow first, then design something that stays reliable under real, continuous load.

On the full-stack side, I pair ASP.NET Core 8 and ASP.NET MVC backends with Angular and AngularJS frontends, and I'm comfortable owning a feature end-to-end — from database schema and stored-procedure tuning through to the live UI a control-room operator relies on.
